How it works & gotchas

The dashboard tries to fetch your public inventory directly. If Steam's CORS policy blocks it (common — Steam doesn't issue CORS headers), you'll get a one-click manual-paste fallback that takes ~5 seconds. Either way, the dashboard diffs against the last snapshot and surfaces new items as a draft session you confirm.

Requires: Public Profile + Public Inventory in Steam privacy settings, and a SteamID64 set in Profile above.

  • Steam can't tell us how an item arrived — case opening vs trade vs purchase looks identical to the API. You tag the source when confirming the diff.
  • Removed items aren't auto-counted as sales (could be trades, trade-ups, or stash moves). Sales come from the Steam Market history paste below.

Privacy & reliability tradeoffs

When you use someone else's proxy, the requests for your prices and Steam inventory pass through their Cloudflare account.

  • The proxy owner can see in their Cloudflare logs which Steam IDs were synced. Your inventory contents aren't logged, but the request paths are.
  • If the proxy owner deletes the Worker or hits a Skinport ban, your live data stops working until you switch.
  • Your dashboard data (sessions, holdings, settings) lives only in your browser. The proxy never sees your dashboard state — only the upstream requests.

For a friend group this is fine. For broader sharing, deploy your own.

csfloat.com/inventory
Powers → Inventory · Top Holdings
Open CSFloat ↗

What you get

Every item you hold with wear conditions and current market prices. Useful if you want float data or a third-party price snapshot alongside Steam's live prices.

Step-by-step

  1. Click Open CSFloat above — sign in with Steam if prompted
  2. Wait 15–30 seconds for prices to load ($ values appear on each item card)
  3. Click the first item row, then Shift+Click the last — or press Ctrl+A to select all text on the page
  4. Press Ctrl+C, then paste here and tap Parse & Import

Expected format

★ Sport Gloves | Amphibious | Well-Worn | 308.82
★ Ursus Knife | Tiger Tooth (StatTrak™) | Factory New | 177.91
Glock-18 | Pink DDPAT (Souvenir) | Minimal Wear | 10.70
MP7 | Fade | Minimal Wear | 9.39

Notes

  • Wear codes work: FN MW FT WW BS or full names like Factory New
  • Prefix knives and gloves with ★ for gold-tier highlighting
  • StatTrak™ and Souvenir in the item name are parsed and flagged automatically
Claude shortcut

Open your CSFloat inventory page, press Ctrl+A → Ctrl+C. Paste into Claude (claude.ai) and say:

"Extract the CS2 items from this page. Format as one per line: Item Name | Wear | Price"

Copy Claude's output and paste it into the box above.

steamcommunity.com/market/myhistory
Powers → Cash Flow · Trade Ledger
Open Market History ↗

What you get

Your full CS2 buy and sell history — cases, skins, keys. Powers your cash flow chart and biggest trade table.

Method A — JSON (recommended · no scrolling needed)

  1. Make sure you're logged into Steam in this browser
  2. Open this URL directly: market/myhistory/render/?count=500¤cy=1
  3. The page will show raw JSON text — press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C
  4. Paste into the box above and tap Parse & Import — format is auto-detected
  5. If you have 500+ transactions, repeat with ?start=500&count=500, then ?start=1000, etc.

Method B — Text (copy from the page)

  1. Open the Market History page (button above) and sign in
  2. Scroll all the way to the bottom — keep clicking "Show more" until it stops. Partial scroll = missing transactions
  3. Ctrl+A → Ctrl+C to copy all visible text
  4. Paste into the box above → Parse & Import

Text format (if typing or reformatting manually)

BOUGHT | Revolution Case | Apr 12 2026 | 0.53
SOLD | StatTrak™ Dual Berettas | Apr 9 2026 | 6.19

How it categorizes

  • Items with Case / Capsule / Package in the name → case spend
  • Other BOUGHT lines → skin / sticker spend
  • SOLD lines → realized sales
  • Top 10 buys and top 10 sales populate the Trade Ledger
Claude shortcut

On the market history page, scroll all the way down, then Ctrl+A → Ctrl+C. Paste into Claude and say:

"Format each CS2 Steam Market transaction as: BOUGHT | Item Name | Date | Price — use SOLD for sales. One per line."

Paste Claude's output into the box above.

Manual entry · this dashboard
Powers → Session Log · Big Hits · auto-derived analytics

What it does

Sessions are the core unit of this dashboard. Each session is one case-opening run, with the pulls you got. From these the dashboard derives your hit rate, big-hit gallery, lifetime cases, and rarity distribution if you didn't paste Lootspectrum data.

Two ways to log a session

  • Add pulls one-by-one — pick the case, type the skin, set wear/rarity/value. Best for sessions of 1–5 pulls or when you want to be precise
  • Bulk paste — drop a multi-line list. Best for unboxing nights with 10+ pulls

Bulk paste format

One pull per line, fields separated by |. Two layouts work:

Item Name | Wear | Rarity | Value
MP9 | Starlight Protector | BS | covert | 60.38
Five-SeveN | Scrawl | BS | milspec | 0.05
G3SG1 | Dream Glade | FT | milspec | 0.63

Or skip the rarity (defaults to milspec):

Item Name | Wear | Value
P2000 | Lifted Spirits | BS | 0.05
MAG-7 | Foresight | FT | 0.07

Field values

  • Wear: FN, MW, FT, WW, BS
  • Rarity: milspec, restricted, classified, covert, knife
  • Value: dollar amount as a plain number (no $ sign needed)

Tips

  • Pulls tagged classified, covert, or knife appear in Big Hits automatically
  • If Live Pricing is on, pasted values get overwritten with current Skinport prices
  • Use the Steam Inventory Sync above for ongoing tracking — it builds the session for you from a diff
